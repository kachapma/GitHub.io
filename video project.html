Description Our purpose is to create a video that promotes a feature of Millersville Campus. My group chose to feature the Art Galleries in Breidenstine Hall, the Art building. As a group we: developed a story board, made a shooting schedule, actually shooting the video, downloaded the raw recording, and then finally edidted on imovie. Our commercials were required to be 60-75 second videos only. The commercial had to have audio, like voice overs, and had to have three scenes with title and credits. We were also graded on how smooth our transitions were.
