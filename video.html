Commercial LinkMillersville University Galleries

The purpose of this commercial project is to create a video that promotes a feature of Millersville Campus. My group chose to feature the Art Galleries in Breidenstine Hall, the Art building. We had to collaborate as a group to come up with the topic of our commercial and then develop a plan. We designated individuals into various roles, someone was the storyboard artist another picked camera angles, someone else edited, and so forth, there were four of us in total in charge of making this minute and a half video. So, as a group we: developed a story board, made a shooting schedule, actually shot the commercial, downloaded the raw recording, and then finally edited our footage on iMovie. Our commercials were required to be 60-75 second videos only. The commercial had to have audio, like voice overs, and had to have three scenes with title and credits. We were also graded on how smooth our transitions were and how complex of video angles we shot in.
